About
Mark'd

Mark'd Studios is a digital creative agency built around one idea: branding, design and performance should never be separated. We plan the strategy, build the visuals, manage the analytics and build the website itself, so every piece is working from the same plan instead of being stitched together after the fact.

Every project blends analytics with storytelling, so results stay measurable and messaging still feels human. That is the promise behind Mark'd: creativity with a strategy behind it, and a website built to prove it.

Makeda Molosi

Mark'd Studios was founded by Makeda Molosi, a digital marketing specialist with hands on experience across SEO, CRM systems and web content, including in house roles at Wella Company and Victory Art.
